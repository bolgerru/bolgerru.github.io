RaceFlow

A team racing regatta is a round robin of short races, often more than a hundred of them. RaceFlow schedules them, times them and publishes the results as they happen, so nobody has to shout them across the dinghy park.

The scheduling system behind Irish team racing

I spotted the gap from inside the sport, built the fix, and now license it to clubs. It has run 19 regattas since August 2025.

Every regatta in one place

First Not Last

An online dinghy racing game with an umpire in every race. It is on the App Store, on Android, on Windows, Mac and Linux, and in the browser. One account keeps your rating across all of them.

Real sailors, real rules

Match races and team races, judged live on the Racing Rules of Sailing. You steer, the sails trim themselves, and fouls are cleared with penalty turns, the same way umpired racing works on the water.

Ranked racing, a leaderboard every night, and seven lessons if you have never held a tiller. Then 1v1, or teams of two or three.

Godot 4 / GDScript / Nakama / Docker

firstnotlast.site

Six places to play

iPhone and iPad on the App Store. Android, Windows, Mac and Linux. In the browser, nothing to install, and you race the same people as the apps.

A leaderboard every night

From 21:00 to 22:00 Irish time the online modes become one Daily Racing queue. Three races in that hour put you on the night's board.

Seven lessons, then live boats

Steering, wind, penalty turns and match-race starts. The lessons need no account. Online racing is against other sailors.
